# HindTechGroup Logo Generation Prompt

Create a premium, modern and futuristic corporate logo for **HindTechGroup**.

Design a distinctive **abstract geometric symbol that subtly forms the letters H, T and G without showing the letters directly**. Use interlocking/negative-space shapes to suggest forward movement, growth, technology and connectivity. The symbol should feel like one intelligent mark rather than three obvious letters.

Use a clean hexagonal/forward-motion structure, strong negative space and balanced geometry. It must remain recognizable at very small sizes.

Brand:
- Name: HindTechGroup
- Tagline: Technology That Moves Forward.
- Established: Since 2017 (About/history only)

Business areas:
- Software development
- Healthcare / Pharma
- EV charging technology

Style:
- Premium
- Minimal
- Geometric
- Futuristic but timeless
- Corporate
- Intelligent
- Vector-like
- Highly scalable

Colours:
- Deep green / emerald
- Mint green accent
- Near-black charcoal
- White

Do not use generic globe, brain, circuit-board, medical cross, EV plug, stock icon or obvious H/T/G lettering.

Create:
1. Horizontal logo: symbol + HindTechGroup
2. Symbol-only mark
3. Stacked logo
4. Square app/favicon icon
5. Monochrome version
6. Transparent-background version

The final symbol must work independently for favicon, app icon, website, social profile, stationery and product branding.
